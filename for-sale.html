Full spec sheet: 2026 GTX Pro 130

The 2026 Sea-Doo GTX Pro 130 is engineered for rental and commercial duty. It prioritizes durability, stability and low maintenance, built on the proven ST3 hull with a naturally aspirated engine.

Engine & Performance

  • Rotax 1630 ACE, 130 HP. Naturally aspirated 3-cylinder tuned for fuel efficiency and long-term reliability under high-hour operation, with iTC electronic throttle control.
  • iBR (Intelligent Brake & Reverse). Precise low-speed maneuvering and shorter stopping distances. Every ski we sell has it.
  • ECO mode. Optimizes power delivery for maximum fuel economy while cruising.

Hull & Durability

  • ST3 fiberglass hull. Deep-V design, stable and predictable in choppy water, a smoother ride than entry-level Polytec hulls.
  • Heavy-duty components. Reinforced wear ring, stainless steel drive shaft and impeller, heavy-duty electrical harness and handle grips.
  • Rental-grade seat. Thermoformed vinyl that resists tearing and sun degradation, with a passenger seat strap.

Storage & Utility

  • 98.9 L (26.1 gal) total storage. Large 96 L front bin plus a glove box with a watertight phone compartment.
  • LinQ attachment system. Tool-less mounting for fuel caddies, coolers and cargo bags on the rear deck.
  • Rear boarding ladder. Standard, for easy re-entry from the water.

Full spec sheet: 24RFX LE 2.75

The Berkshire 24RFX LE 2.75 is a value-focused tritoon that bridges the gap between entry-level two-tube pontoons and high-end triple-tube boats. The 2.75 designation refers to its performance tube package: 25-inch outer pontoons plus a 3/4-length center sport tube for better lift, stability and handling, especially in turns or under a full load.

Performance & Hull

  • Tube configuration. Two full-length 25-inch outer tubes and a 25-inch center sport tube running about 75% of the hull, adding stern buoyancy, reducing squat under acceleration and improving planing.
  • Lifting strakes & underdeck. Large interior lifting strakes on the outer tubes for speed and fuel economy, with a full aluminum underdeck protecting the floor and stiffening the frame.
  • SeaStar hydraulic steering. With tilt helm. Smoother and more responsive than the cable steering on base models.
  • Power. Rated for up to 150 HP, paired here with a 2024 Mercury 115hp four-stroke.

Layout & Amenities (RFX floorplan)

  • Rear-facing stern lounge. The signature RFX layout lets passengers face backward, ideal for sunbathing or watching swimmers.
  • Seats 12. Max weight capacity 1,785 lbs across forward and aft benches plus the stern lounge.
  • LE trim. Bimini top, stainless steel ski tow bar, LED docking lights and a trolling motor outlet.
  • Storage. Large in-floor compartment (approx. 24 x 79 in) and a lockable helm glove box.
  • Construction. Z42 wood-free composite deck to prevent rot, covered in durable woven vinyl flooring.
